# Concept C — Premium Minimalist Glass (UI/UX Pro Max)

Source: `design-system/easyeap-concept-c/MASTER.md`. Query "premium minimal dark saas admin", density 4, variance 2, motion 2. Accepted: Minimalism & Swiss style ("clean, spacious, functional, high contrast, grid-based"), effects "subtle hover 200–250ms, clear type hierarchy", palette notes "premium black + gold accent". Rejected: light background (brief pins dark), Cormorant/Montserrat and Playfair (luxury fashion), the scroll-storytelling page pattern. Typography retry "enterprise professional saas clean" → Plus Jakarta Sans accepted.

- **Shell:** no sidebar. A single top glass navigation bar with section menus (Overview, Identity, Certificates, Network, System); the **tenant is the root of the breadcrumb**, a popover switcher; content centred at max ≈1200px.
- **Dashboard:** one headline posture statement ("Harborview Logistics is healthy — 2 things need attention"), the attention list, then three large metrics with context, then one wide chart.
- **Cards & glass:** almost no cards — sections separated by space and hairlines; glass only on the nav bar and popovers; 14px radius sheets.
- **Type:** Plus Jakarta Sans; large light headings (28–40px, 300–400), 15px body, generous line height.
- **Tables:** borderless list rows (56px), no vertical rules, inline row expansion for detail.
- **Forms/dialogs:** full-page focused flows (e.g. add controller is its own page with a left progress column); small centred sheets for confirmations.
- **Colour:** ground #0E0E10, surface #141417, hairline white/7; text #F4F1EA / #B9B4AA / #8E8A82; accent champagne #D4A85A (UUPM gold #A16207 lightened for dark contrast) used sparingly for primary action + focus.
- **Signature move:** one sentence leads every screen — the posture statement on the dashboard and a one-line summary at the top of each list ("38 of 42 people can join Wi-Fi").
